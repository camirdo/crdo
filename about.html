ABOUT

I’m an Argentinian sound designer, researcher and educator working across film, sound and social research, currently based in France. I studied Sound Direction at ENERC (Escuela Nacional de Experimentación y Realización Cinematográfica) and hold a degree in Audiovisual Communication from Universidad Nacional de San Martín (UNSAM).

Alongside my practice in cinema, I’m interested in sound as a way of exploring social experience. My current independent research focuses on listening, sound identities and sound ethnography, connecting anthropology and social sciences with audiovisual and documentary work.

I also worked in education, sharing my experience in sound with students and emerging cinematographers.
