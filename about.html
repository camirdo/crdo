ABOUT

I’m an argentinian sound designer, researcher and educator working across film, sound and social research currently based in France.

Alongside my practice in cinema, I’m interested in sound as a way of exploring social experience. My current independent research focuses on listening, sound identities and sound ethnography, connecting antropology and social sciences with audiovisual and documentary work.

I also worked in education, sharing my experience in sound with students and emerging cinematographers.
